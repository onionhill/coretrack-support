# KidsDraw Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 22, 2026_

KidsDraw is made for young children, so it is built to collect nothing.

## What we collect

Nothing. KidsDraw does not collect, transmit, or share any data, and it
does not collect personal information from children or anyone else.

## Where your child's data lives

Everything made in KidsDraw — drawings, colorings, and stickers — is
stored locally on the iPad. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no
advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

## Photos access

If you choose "Save to Photos", KidsDraw asks for add-only access to
the Photos library. This permission only lets the app add the finished
drawing; it can never read, browse, or modify existing photos.

## In-app purchase

KidsDraw offers a single one-time purchase that unlocks all coloring
packs, placed behind a parental gate so children cannot buy it
themselves. The payment is processed entirely by Apple through the App
Store; KidsDraw never sees names, payment details, or any other
personal information. The only network communication in the app is
this StoreKit exchange with Apple. Apple's handling of purchase data
is described in [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Deleting your data

Delete individual drawings from the in-app gallery at any time.
Deleting the app from the iPad removes everything it stored.

## Third parties

KidsDraw shares no data with third parties. Apart from Apple's App
Store purchase processing described above, the app makes no network
connections.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of KidsDraw ever changes how data is handled, this
policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change
will be clearly communicated in the app.
