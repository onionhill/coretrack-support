# Pengerom Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 18, 2026_

This app is now called **Pengerom**. It was called Fynn during development.
Read the current policy here:
[Pengerom privacy policy](../pengerom/privacy-policy.html).

The short version stays the same. Pengerom collects nothing. It has no account,
analytics, advertising, tracking SDKs, or server. Pengerom makes no network
connection, so nothing you enter can leave your device.

Pengerom's coach is built into the app and it is deterministic. It reads your
words with rules written into the app, and it calculates every figure on your
device. The coach uses no AI service.
