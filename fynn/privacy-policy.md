# Fynn Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 24, 2026_

Fynn is built so that your money stays your business.

## What we collect

Nothing. Fynn has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking
SDKs, and no server of its own. Nothing you enter is transmitted to us,
because there is nowhere for it to go.

## Where your data lives

Everything you enter — goals, logged entries, income, planned expenses,
categories, challenges, manual accounts, balance snapshots, your chat
history and the coach's notes about you — is stored locally on your
device in Fynn's own storage. That storage is shared with Fynn's own
Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and its Siri shortcut, so they can
show and update the same figures. It is not shared with any other app.

## The AI coach

Fynn's coach runs on **Apple's on-device Foundation Models** — the
Apple Intelligence model that ships with iOS. Your messages and your
financial context are processed on your iPhone by the operating system's
model. Fynn does not use OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or any other AI
service, holds no API keys, and makes no network requests to generate a
reply. If your device doesn't support Apple Intelligence, Fynn falls
back to a simple built-in assistant that runs entirely in the app.

All financial figures the coach mentions are computed by Fynn's own
code, not by the model.

## Voice dictation (optional)

If you tap the microphone in the chat, Fynn uses Apple's Speech
framework to turn your speech into text. Transcription is performed by
iOS, not by Fynn, and Fynn **asks iOS to keep it on your device**
whenever your device and language support on-device recognition. Where
on-device recognition is not available — some languages and older
devices — iOS falls back to processing the audio on Apple's servers,
which is governed by [Apple's privacy
policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Fynn only receives the
resulting text, and only while you hold the mic active. You never have
to use it — typing does exactly the same thing.

## Notifications (optional)

Milestone nudges, bill reminders and the weekly check-in are **local**
notifications, scheduled on your device by your device. They are off
until you turn them on, and no push server is involved.

## Sharing

The only time anything leaves the app is when you deliberately share it
— for example tapping Share on your "Year in Money" card, which hands
an image to iOS's share sheet for you to send wherever you choose.

## Deleting your data

- **Settings → Clear chat history** deletes the stored conversation.
- **Settings → What Fynn remembers → Forget everything** deletes the
  coach's notes about you.
- Goals, bills, income, categories and accounts can be deleted
  individually where you created them.
- Deleting Fynn from your device removes all of its data permanently.

## Children

Fynn is a personal finance tool for general audiences. It collects no
data from anyone, including children.

## Third parties

Fynn shares no data with third parties. It has no third-party SDKs, no
trackers, and no network access for your data to travel over.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of Fynn ever changes how data is handled — for
example an optional iCloud sync — this policy will be updated before
that version is released, and the change will be made clear in the app.
