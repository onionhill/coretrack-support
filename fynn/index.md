# Fynn — Support

Fynn is a private, on-device money coach for iPhone. You talk to it in
plain language — "paid 400 on my Amex", "what can I spend today?" — and
it tracks your savings goals, debt payoff, budget and net worth. No
account, no cloud, no ads.

## Frequently asked

**Where is my data stored?**
Entirely on your iPhone. Fynn has no account and no server. Your goals,
entries, budget, challenges and chat history never leave the device.

**Is the AI coach sending my finances to a server?**
No. The coach runs on Apple's on-device Foundation Models — the same
Apple Intelligence model built into iOS. Your messages are processed
locally on your iPhone. Fynn has no API key and talks to no AI service.

**The chat says my coach isn't available — why?**
The on-device model needs an Apple Intelligence–capable iPhone with
Apple Intelligence enabled in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri.
Without it, Fynn falls back to a simpler built-in assistant that still
logs entries and answers basic questions, but with less natural
language understanding.

**Where do the numbers come from — can the AI make them up?**
Every figure is calculated by Fynn itself in ordinary code: progress,
payoff dates, interest, safe-to-spend, recaps. The coach is only allowed
to put those numbers into words. If it doesn't have a figure, it is
instructed to say so rather than guess.

**How do I log something without chatting?**
Open a goal and tap **Log progress**, use the quick-log Home Screen
widget, or ask Siri ("Log a payment in Fynn"). All of them write the
same entry.

**How do I fix or delete an entry?**
Open the goal and use its History list. Deleting a goal removes its
entries with it.

**What does "Safe to spend" actually mean?**
Your monthly income, minus your planned bills, minus what your goals
need this month to stay on track — divided by the days left in the
month. Set up income and expenses in the Budget tab to make it accurate.

**What is "What Fynn remembers"?**
The coach keeps a short list of durable notes about you (for example
"gets paid on the 25th") so it doesn't ask the same thing twice. You can
read every note, swipe any of them away, or delete them all in
Settings → What Fynn remembers. Amounts and balances are never stored
as notes — those always come from your live data.

**How do I delete my data?**
Settings has **Clear chat history** and **Forget everything** (coach
notes). Individual goals, bills and accounts can be deleted where you
created them. Deleting the app removes absolutely everything.

**Does Fynn work offline?**
Yes, completely. There is nothing to be online for.

**Why does it ask for the microphone?**
Only for optional voice dictation in the chat, and only when you tap the
mic button. Typing works exactly as well — see the privacy policy for
how transcription is handled.
