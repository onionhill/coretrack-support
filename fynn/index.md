# Pengerom — Support

_Last updated: August 18, 2026_

This app is now called **Pengerom**. It was called Fynn during development.
The current pages are here:

- Support: [Pengerom support](../pengerom/)
- Privacy: [Pengerom privacy policy](../pengerom/privacy-policy.html)

Pengerom is a private money coach for iPhone. It helps you record savings and
debt payments, follow goals, plan a budget, and view your net worth. Pengerom
has no account, cloud service, or ads.

## About the coach

Pengerom's coach is built into the app and it is deterministic. It reads your
words with rules written into the app, and it calculates every figure on your
device. The coach uses no AI service. Pengerom makes no network connection.
