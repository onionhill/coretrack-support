# CoreTrack: Weight & Body — Support

CoreTrack is a private, on-device weight and body measurement tracker
for iPhone.

## Frequently asked

**Where is my data stored?**
Entirely on your device. CoreTrack has no account, no cloud, and makes
no network connections.

**How do I fix a wrong entry?**
Tap the entry in Recent History (Home) or Recent Activity (Trends) to
edit its value or date, or delete it.

**How do I delete all my data?**
Profile → Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes everything.

**How do I switch between kg/lbs or cm/inches?**
Profile → Units.
