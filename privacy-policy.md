# CoreTrack Privacy Policy

_Last updated: June 12, 2026_

CoreTrack is built so that your data stays yours.

## What we collect

Nothing. CoreTrack does not collect, transmit, or share any data.

## Where your data lives

Everything you enter in CoreTrack — weight entries, body measurements,
goals, and profile details — is stored locally on your device. The app
makes no network connections, requires no account, and contains no
analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

## Deleting your data

You can permanently delete all data at any time from
Profile → Delete all data. Deleting the app from your device also
removes all stored data.

## Third parties

CoreTrack shares no data with third parties, because it has no data
to share and no network access to share it over.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of CoreTrack ever changes how data is handled
(for example, an optional cloud sync feature), this policy will be
updated before that version is released, and the change will be
clearly communicated in the app.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at
https://github.com/onionhill/CoreTrack/issues
