# Pengerom Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 18, 2026_

Pengerom is built so that your money stays your business.

## What we collect

Nothing. Pengerom has no account, analytics, advertising, tracking SDKs, or
server. Nothing you enter is sent to us. Pengerom makes no network connection
of any kind, so your information has nowhere to go.

## Where your data lives

Pengerom stores your goals, logged entries, income, planned expenses,
categories, challenges, accounts, balance snapshots, chat history, and your
settings on your device. Its Home Screen widgets, Lock Screen widgets, and Siri
shortcut use the same local data. No other app can use this data. Pengerom has
no iCloud sync, so nothing is copied to another device.

## The coach

Pengerom has a built-in coach that recognizes supported money updates and
answers basic questions about your plan. The coach is deterministic: it reads
your words with rules written into the app, and it always shows you a change
before Pengerom saves it. Pengerom calculates every figure on your device.
Pengerom uses no AI service, holds no API key, and sends your financial data
nowhere.

## Voice dictation (optional)

If you tap the microphone in the coach, Pengerom uses Apple's Speech framework
to turn speech into text. Pengerom requests on-device transcription when your
device and language support it. Apple can process audio on its servers when
on-device recognition is unavailable. Apple's privacy policy applies in that
case. Pengerom only receives the resulting text while you use dictation.
Typing always works without the microphone.

## What Pengerom never uses

Pengerom asks for the microphone and speech recognition only, and only for
dictation. It does not use your health data, contacts, location, photos, or
camera. It contains no advertising identifier and no third-party identifier.

## Notifications (optional)

Pengerom schedules reminders and weekly check-ins on your device. They stay
off until you enable them. No push server is involved.

## Sharing

Data leaves Pengerom only when you choose to share it. For example, you can
share a Year in Money image through the iOS share sheet.

## Deleting your data

- **Settings → Clear chat history** deletes the stored conversation.
- You can delete goals, bills, income, categories, and accounts individually.
- Deleting Pengerom from your device removes its local data permanently.

## Children

Pengerom is a general-audience personal finance tool. It collects no data from
children or adults.

## Third parties

Pengerom shares no data with third parties. It has no third-party SDKs,
trackers, or network service for your financial data.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how Pengerom handles data, this policy will be
updated before that version is released.
