# Pengerom — Support

Pengerom is a private money coach for iPhone. It helps you record savings
and debt payments, follow goals, plan a budget, and view your net worth.
Pengerom has no account, cloud service, or ads.

## Frequently asked

**Where is my data stored?**
Pengerom stores your goals, entries, budget, challenges, accounts, and chat
history on your iPhone. The app has no account and no server.

**Does Pengerom send my finances to a server?**
No. Pengerom uses a built-in coach on your iPhone. It does not use an AI
service or send your financial data to a server.

**Where do the numbers come from?**
Pengerom calculates progress, payoff dates, interest, safe-to-spend, and
recaps in the app. The coach only explains those figures.

**How do I log something without chatting?**
Open a goal and tap **Log progress**. You can also use the Home Screen
widget or ask Siri to log a payment.

**How do I fix or delete an entry?**
Open the goal and use its History list. Deleting a goal also deletes its
entries.

**What does "Safe to spend" mean?**
It is your monthly income, minus planned bills and goal amounts, divided by
the days left in the month. Add income and expenses in the Budget tab.

**How do I delete my data?**
Use **Clear chat history** to delete the conversation. You can delete goals,
bills, income, categories, and accounts where you create them. Deleting the
app removes its local data.

**Does Pengerom work offline?**
Yes. Pengerom works offline.

**Why does Pengerom ask for the microphone?**
The microphone is only for optional voice dictation when you tap the mic
button. Typing works without microphone access.
